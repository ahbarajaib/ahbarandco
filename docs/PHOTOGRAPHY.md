# Ahbar & Co. — Photography Direction

Photography is the most important brand asset. The website exists to showcase the furniture — images should dominate every product and collection page.

## Style

- Natural daylight
- Warm sunlight
- Soft shadows
- Minimal interiors
- Neutral walls
- Real homes
- Clean compositions

## Materials to Feature

- Walnut
- Oak
- Leather
- Linen
- Natural fabrics
- Stone accents

## Avoid

- Neon colors
- Heavy filters
- Studio glamour shots
- Overstyled scenes
- Extremely dark backgrounds
- Heavy luxury aesthetics

## Image Types

### Lifestyle
Full-room or contextual shots showing furniture in calm, modern living spaces. Warm light, neutral palette, minimal styling.

### Detail
Close-ups of joinery, wood grain, sanding marks, finishing, and hardware. Communicates craftsmanship.

### Product
Clean product shots on neutral backgrounds for catalog/grid views. Consistent angles and lighting across a collection.

### Process
Workshop photography — hands at work, tools, raw materials, finishing. Used in About and Craftsmanship sections.

## Technical Guidelines

| Context | Aspect Ratio | Min Width |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Hero | 16:9 or 3:2 | 2400px |
| Collection card | 4:5 | 1200px |
| Product gallery | 1:1 or 4:5 | 1600px |
| Journal cover | 3:2 | 1600px |
| Detail/macro | 4:3 | 1200px |

- Format: WebP preferred, JPEG fallback
- Storage: Cloudflare R2
- Serve via Next.js `<Image>` with responsive sizes
- Alt text: descriptive, include material and product name

## Mood Reference

The brand mood is inspired by premium furniture photography:

- Warm walnut wood tones
- Natural shadows
- Soft sunlight
- Premium craftsmanship
- Quiet confidence
- Rich textures
- Elegant typography in scene styling

The website interface remains bright and product-focused — photography provides warmth, not the UI chrome.
