# Ahbar & Co. — Shippable Minimalist Product Catalog Proposal

This document outlines a comprehensive blueprint of **6 collections** containing **4 products each** (24 products total). All items are designed specifically for a sole-proprietor woodworker operating without heavy industrial machinery. 

### Core Shop Blueprint Constraints:
* **Tooling**: Can be made using standard handheld power tools (track saw, router, orbital sander, drill) and light benchtop tools (miter saw, jobsite table saw, benchtop planer).
* **Shipping**: Compact, lightweight, or flat-packable to optimize online sales margins and keep shipping costs low.
* **Aesthetic**: Modern Warm Minimalism, premium hardwoods (White Oak, Walnut, Maple), brass/leather accents, and tactile oil finishes.

## 1. The Workspace Collection ("Architectural Desktop")
*Focus: Desktop organization, tech integration, premium office goods.*

### 1.1 The Walnut Desk Shelf / Monitor Riser (Flat-pack)
* **Concept**: A premium monitor stand that raises screens to eye level and hides keyboards underneath.
* **Construction**: Table saw or track saw for the top shelf. Legs are cut from matching stock. Use threaded brass inserts under the shelf and screw-in legs/supports for flat-pack shipping.
* **Wood Choice**: Solid American Walnut or Rift-Sawn White Oak.
* **Pricing Tier**: Premium ($120 - $180).

### 1.2 The Qi-Charging Valet Tray
* **Concept**: A desk tray for keys, pens, and coins with a hidden wireless charger embedded underneath the wood.
* **Construction**: Use a handheld router with a template guide and a bowl-and-tray bit to scoop the tray compartments. Flip the piece and use a Forstner bit to drill a pocket from the underside, leaving only 2-3mm of wood thickness for the Qi coil to pass power.
* **Wood Choice**: Solid Maple or Cherry.
* **Pricing Tier**: Mid-High ($85 - $115).

### 1.3 The Vertical Laptop Stand
* **Concept**: A heavy, weighted vertical slot stand that holds a closed laptop upright, clearing desk space.
* **Construction**: Dado cuts on the table saw to create a clean slot. Line the inner slot with premium adhesive merino wool felt.
* **Wood Choice**: Walnut or White Oak.
* **Pricing Tier**: Mid ($65 - $80).

### 1.4 The Desktop Pen & Card Caddy
* **Concept**: A heavy wooden block with angled drilling for pens and a sliced slot for business cards, with a solid brass accent baseplate.
* **Construction**: Drill press (or drilling guide) with various Forstner bits at a 15-degree angle. Table saw cut for the card slot. Add a heavy solid brass plate to the bottom for weight and a premium feel.
* **Wood Choice**: Walnut body with a raw brass base.
* **Pricing Tier**: Giftable ($45 - $60).

## Sole-Proprietor Production & Efficiency Strategy

1. **Batching**: Never build one item at a time. Group your tasks: spend Monday milling wood for 10 stands, Tuesday routing all of them, Wednesday sanding, Thursday applying finish, and Friday packing/shipping.
2. **Jig Everything**: Build jigs for your table saw and router. A spline jig, crosscut sled, and router templates ensure safety, high accuracy, and speed up production by 5x.
3. **Hardwax Finishes**: Avoid spray finishes or complex varnishes. Use hardwax oils (like **Rubio Monocoat** or **Osmo Polyx-Oil**). They apply with a buffing pad in a single coat, dry quickly, are completely food-safe, and give the wood a premium, natural tactile feel.
